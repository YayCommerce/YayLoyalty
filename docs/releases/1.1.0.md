# YayLoyalty 1.1.0

YayLoyalty grows from points for orders into a full loyalty program: VIP tiers, referrals, more ways to earn, and free shipping and free product rewards your customers can redeem.

## New

- **VIP tiers (Pro).** Place customers into tiers by the points they earn: over their lifetime, in a rolling window of 3 to 24 months, or each calendar month, quarter or year. Each tier can multiply the points customers earn and pay a one-time bonus when they reach it. Redeeming points never lowers a tier. Customers see their tier and how far they are from the next one in My Account and the launcher, and you can filter and sort customers by tier.
- **Referral program (Pro).** Every customer gets a referral link. A friend who signs up through it gets a discount on their first order, and the referrer earns points once that order completes and the hold period you choose has passed. Self-referrals and refunded orders earn nothing. A new Referral screen lists every referral and lets you approve or reject the ones on hold, and customers follow their invitations on a Referrals tab in My Account.
- **Match any or all conditions (Pro).** A campaign or reward can now apply when any of its conditions match, for example a lifetime spend over a set amount *or* at least five orders.
- **Social campaigns (Pro).** Award points once for following or sharing your store on Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn or Pinterest. Each one shows in Ways to earn.
- **Subscription renewal campaigns (Pro).** With WooCommerce Subscriptions active, award points for each renewal order, on top of order points or instead of them. A refunded renewal takes its points back.
- **Free shipping and free product rewards (Pro).** Customers can now redeem them in the classic and block cart and checkout. Free shipping brings every shipping rate to zero; a free product is added to the cart at no cost. When a reward can't be used, the cart says why, and cancelling or refunding the order returns the points.
- **Page builder widgets.** Add the points balance, ways to earn, ways to redeem and the redeem card to pages built with Elementor, Bricks or Divi. Sites without a page builder load none of this code.
- **Tools.** Import point balances from a CSV file, with a preview and an error for each row before anything is saved; importing the same file twice never adds points twice. Export every customer's balance to CSV.

## Improved

- Storefront output and emails are escaped more strictly.
- The plugin stops with a notice on WordPress versions it doesn't support.
- Pro: the loyalty storefront (the launcher, My Account tabs and the redeem card) stays hidden until the license is activated. Customers still earn points in the meantime.

## Fixed

- Removing all coupons from a block cart now also removes the points discount.
- A date filter with an impossible date, such as 30 February, no longer filters from the following month.
- The free version's admin page no longer shows the Pro name.
